The concept, in words (contract v11)
  1. The slide tracks the finger 1:1 — no easing between finger and pane.
  2. The colour blend is linear and completes at 2× the commit threshold — colour = "the switch is arming", half-done exactly at the commit point.
  3. A stationary finger holds the slide — event silence is never gesture-over.
  4. Resumed movement continues the same gesture from where it held.
  5. Commit or cancel is decided only by a real release (or a decisive interrupt) — never by a timer. A 10 s abandoned gesture rolls back, never commits.
  6. Past a full slide, a spring overshoot shows the gesture continuing; release springs back to the target space.
  7. No frame composites both spaces' content misaligned.
  8. One event owner per window.
  9. A fast flick commits with a visible transition.
  10. A spurious mid-gesture end cannot swallow a live gesture (80 ms revoke window).
  11. A decisive non-scroll input (a click) finishes the gesture through the normal decision: forward past the threshold, back home below it.
  12. Endpoint saturation resolves instantly: once the slide is pinned at an endpoint (full overshoot, or a rail end) and input still pushes toward it, further movement carries no intent — commit / bounce NOW and swallow the rest of that gesture's same-direction input.
